04 Arrival — Get · Into · Orbit

  

  
POL-1 revised · Get fades → Into fades → O R B I T lands · 2s sweep crosses the wordmark · then the room is lit — once per launch, NO_ANIMATION skips

  
    
    
  • The fades are colour ramps, not motion: each word steps from near-background to
        full ink and back over a handful of frames — glyphs never move, brightness is the only
        animated quantity, which truecolor terminals do perfectly. On a 16-colour terminal it
        degrades to appear/disappear, still correct.
    • 
    
  • The sweep is a soft highlight travelling across the wordmark's cells over 2s —
        per-cell colour lift toward gold-white, left to right, then gone. Again: colour only,
        cell-truthful.
    • 
    
  • Then ⟡, identity, menu and foot are simply there. Total ≈ 6s once per launch;
        ORBIT_LAUNCHER_NO_ANIMATION (and any keypress) skips straight to the lit room.
