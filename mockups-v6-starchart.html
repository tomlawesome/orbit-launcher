orbit-launcher · v6 — starchart alignment

Orbit's web identity (v8 → v9 "the living chart", issue #307) made starchart the primary theme: deep navy, gold accent #d8b45a, soft-blue #8fb8ff for what's approaching, and a twelve-point calendar compass. v9 added nine named polish behaviours (POL-1..9). The launcher's current palette is the web's "afterdark" theme — this set proposes the echo back, plus the two POL behaviours that survive the terminal (panel 04; the audit of all nine is at the foot). Every panel is a cell-truth render: 80×26 fixed-width cells, 10 fps, no effect a real terminal can't produce. The terminal's own background is whatever the user runs — nothing here paints one.

02 Mission console — the stage bar becomes a calendar compass

  

  
install mid-flight · twelve phase ticks, one now-marker, no percentage

  
    
    
  • The idea: the web dial's outer ring is twelve month ticks with a now-marker. The
        engine's canonical phase order is also twelve stations (bootstrap → … → complete).
        The console's plain bar becomes that compass laid flat: passed ticks
        gold, the current station a ◆ now-marker,
        the road ahead faint. Fill is still engine truth — a tick lights only when its phase is
        reached. Still no percentage, ever.
    • 
    
  • Cell cost: one render function on the existing bar row. Ticks are ─ ┼ ◆ — no
        exotic glyphs, degrades to ASCII (- + *) cleanly.
    • 
    
  • Unchanged: frame, event grammar (✓ / spinner / ✗), clock, stage word, raw-prose
        fallback for legacy engines.
    •

04 The living chart, terminal edition — the two POL survivors

  

    

      

      
POL-1 arrival · the mark lands, the wordmark unfolds — once per launch, ~1.2s

    

    

      

      
POL-7 restored orbit · on success, the gold body drifts one orbit outward, brief trail

    

  

  
    
    
  • Arrival: ⟡ appears alone for a beat, then the three wordmark rows land top-to-bottom,
        then identity and menu. One shot per launch; ORBIT_LAUNCHER_NO_ANIMATION skips it
        (same reduced-motion rule as the web). Twelve frames of things appearing — no easing to
        fake, nothing a cell can't do.
    • 
    
  • Restored orbit: entering the success screen, the binary's gold body steps outward to
        a wider orbit over ~1.2s, leaving three fading trail dots, then settles — the web's comet,
        reduced to one glyph and the truth that a completed thing moves to a calmer orbit. Runs
        once; the loop here is mockup-only.
